Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

ChessArena is a free online chess application. It does not offer wallets, deposits, withdrawals, or cash prizes.

Information we collect

  • Account data: email, username, phone number (for OTP), profile photo if you upload one.
  • Gameplay data: matches, moves, Elo rating, friends, challenges, in-game chat.
  • Device and notification data: FCM token and basic device info used for security and push notifications.

How we use it

We use this data to run matches, ranking, friend challenges, notifications, abuse reports, and account security. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

We use Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Messaging) to operate the app. We may share data if required by law or to investigate abuse reports.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or use the account deletion page.
