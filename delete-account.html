Delete your account

Google Play requires a public way to request account deletion.

In the app

  1. Open ChessArena and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your Firebase Auth user and associated profile data we store.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email support@chessarena.app from the address on your account. Include “Delete my ChessArena account” and we will process the request.

Some backup logs may remain for a limited time for security and abuse investigation, then they are deleted.
